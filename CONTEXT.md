# Laura's chore tracker

A personal chore tracker for Laura's routine in a long-stay hotel, accompanied by an animated cartoon red panda.

## Language

**Chore**:
A recurring household activity Laura manages, such as changing bedding, taking bins down, cleaning the bathroom or room, or washing lights or darks.

**Rotation**:
A repeating two-week timetable made up of Week A and Week B, used for chores with fixed days.
_Avoid_: A fortnight measured from the last completion

**Completion-based timing**:
Scheduling a chore relative to when it was last completed, rather than on a fixed day of the rotation.

**Today**:
The main view showing the chores relevant to Laura's current day.

**Companion**:
The cartoon red panda that provides little celebrations when Laura makes progress.

**Still to do**:
Unfinished chores from an earlier day, shown separately from today's scheduled chores.
_Avoid_: Failure list

**Move**:
Rescheduling one chore occurrence to a day Laura chooses, without changing future scheduled dates.

**Skip this time**:
Dismissing one occurrence of a chore without completing it or removing the recurring chore.

**Laundry cycle**:
A lights or darks washing chore with three steps: Wash, Dry, and Put away. Progress is preserved between steps; finishing the full cycle counts as one completed chore.

**Date invitation**:
A bonus reward revealed after every 10 completed chores while an invitation is waiting, offering a real date together using a supplied message and plan. An invitation remains available until marked used, with no expiry.

**Upcoming**:
A look ahead covering today and the following thirteen days of Laura's routine.
